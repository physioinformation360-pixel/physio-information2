Comprehensive Physiotherapy Care

At Physio Information, we provide evidence-based, patient-centered physiotherapy services designed for recovery, performance, and long-term health.

🦴 Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Treatment for neck pain, back pain, joint stiffness, arthritis, postural issues, and work-related musculoskeletal disorders.

🏃 Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Specialized rehabilitation programs for sports injuries including ligament injuries, muscle strains, and return-to-sport training.

🧠 Neuro Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for stroke, spinal cord injuries, neurological conditions, and balance & coordination training.

🦵 Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Pre and post-operative rehabilitation for fractures, joint replacements, and orthopedic surgeries.

💪 Pain Management

Evidence-based pain management techniques including exercise therapy, manual therapy, and patient education.

📘 Patient Education & Exercise Training

Teaching correct posture, ergonomics, home exercise programs, and lifestyle modifications for long-term recovery.
